FAQ

Common questions about transfers

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We monitor your flight status using the flight number you provide. If your flight is delayed or lands early, we reschedule our driver's dispatch automatically. You do not need to contact us, and there are no extra charges for delays.

Where will I meet the driver?

For international flights, your driver will be waiting in the arrivals hall right outside the customs exit. For domestic flights, they will meet you near the baggage carousels. They will be holding a tablet showing your name so they are easy to spot.

How much waiting time is included?

We include 60 minutes of free waiting time starting from the moment your flight lands. This gives you plenty of time to clear immigration, claim your luggage, and walk to the arrivals terminal.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, we can provide pre-installed child seats (infant capsules, child seats, or booster seats) suitable for children of all ages. Please mention your children's ages in the booking notes so we can select and install the correct seat ahead of time.

What is included in the flat rate?

The flat rate covers the private vehicle, meet-and-greet greeting inside the terminal, and the transfer. Road tolls and airport parking fees are charged separately at cost with receipts after the transfer.

Going outside the CBD or need transfers between regional hubs? Contact us on WhatsApp for an instant custom quote.
