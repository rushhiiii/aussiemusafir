Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is $600 really the total price, not per person?

Yes — $600 AUD covers the car, fuel, and your guide-driver for the whole day, for up to 4 guests. Split it between your group however you like.

What if there are 5 or 6 of us?

We move you into a larger vehicle for $800 flat, still for the whole day, still with a dedicated guide-driver. Just select your group size in the trip request form and we'll confirm the right vehicle.

Can I combine destinations, like Great Ocean Road and Phillip Island?

Not in a single day — they're on opposite sides of Melbourne — but let us know in the custom request form and we'll suggest the best way to fit both into your trip.

Do you book Penguin Parade tickets for the Phillip Island tour?

Yes, we handle the booking once your tour is confirmed. The ticket cost is separate from the $600 tour fare.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your tour. Inside 48 hours, a partial fee may apply — we'll confirm this when you book.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, on request. Mention the number and ages of children in the trip request form.

Is gratuity included?

No, gratuity isn't included in the $600 fare — always appreciated, but entirely up to you.
