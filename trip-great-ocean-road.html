Overview

Great Ocean Road, at your own pace

The Great Ocean Road is best not rushed — this route gives you real time at the Twelve Apostles, a walk through Otway rainforest, and stops the big coaches skip entirely. Your guide-driver adjusts the pace through the day, so a longer stop at one lookout just means a shorter one somewhere else, never a cut corner.

The fine print

What's included, what's not

Included

  • Private car & licensed guide-driver for the full day
  • Hotel pickup & drop-off across Melbourne CBD and inner suburbs
  • Chilled water bottles for every guest
  • First-aid kit onboard
  • All lookout and walking-trail access along the route
  • Flexible pacing — adjust stops on the day

Not included

  • Lunch and any personal food or drinks
  • Gratuities
  • Optional paid activities (e.g. helicopter flights, boat tours)
  • Wine or produce tastings if added to the route
